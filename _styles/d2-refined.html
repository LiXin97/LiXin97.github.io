I build benchmarks that are meant to be audited rather than trusted, post-training that rewards reasoning a verifier can actually check, and measurement for systems where several models talk to each other. WirelessMathBench-XL ships per-problem contamination evidence next to every score; DafnyComp asks whether verification that succeeds locally still succeeds once the pieces have to compose; Re:Form cuts human priors out of RL-trained formal verification; DebateLedger counts the answers multi-agent debate repairs against the ones it quietly destroys. The common thread is a preference for domains where an answer can be checked — formal verification, mathematics, code, and wireless systems.

Before the Ph.D. I worked on robot perception: visual-inertial odometry at MEGVII, RGB-D + IMU indoor mapping at Microsoft Research Asia, and multimodal localization at Gausium Robotics, where I led a team of five engineers and shipped a system running on a fleet of 1,000+ commercial cleaning robots. I hold an M.E. from Peking University and a B.E. from Northeastern University, China. I am always glad to talk about research or collaboration; email is the fastest route.

Research

Selected work

  • Measuring Collapse and Correction in Homogeneous-Panel LLM Debate

    NeurIPS 2026

    Xin Li*, Mengbing Liu*, Chau Yuen

    Across 6,925 MMLU-Pro debates, 253 correct answers collapse. A probe-gated freeze prevents 29 of them but gives up 108 corrections — net −79 under equal weights — so an intervention scored only on collapse prevention looks like a win while making the panel worse.

    Project

  • WirelessMathBench-XL: An Auditable Benchmark for Wireless Mathematical Reasoning

    NeurIPS 2026

    Xin Li, Mengbing Liu, Yiyang Zhu, Wenhe Zhang, Li Wei, Jiancheng An, Chau Yuen

    4,027 problems drawn from 836 arXiv papers across 20 wireless subfields, each shipped with per-problem 13-gram contamination-overlap evidence against public pretraining text. That is a rerunnable audit of one lexical channel, not a proof the set is clean — which is the point: the evidence is published with the score instead of being promised.

    ProjectarXiv

  • Beyond Teacher Assignment: Domain-Normalized Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

    arXiv 2026

    Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Xin Gao, Annan Wang, Yuchen Xie, Jinghao Guo, Xingwei Qu, Yichi Zhang, Chau Yuen

    Routing decides which teacher supervises a prompt, but not how much that teacher's feedback should count. Normalizing each teacher's feedback scale per domain adds 1.2–3.1 points over label routing, across 2B, 4B and 9B students.

    ProjectarXivCodeDataModels

  • Stacked Intelligent Metasurface-Diffractive Deep Neural Networks for Onboard Terrain Classification from SAR Level-0 Raw Data

    IEEE TSP 2026

    Mengbing Liu, Xin Li, Jiancheng An, Chau Yuen

    About 90% accuracy classifying terrain directly from SAR Level-0 raw data, with the stacked metasurface doing the inference in-wave.

    Project

  • TransPathNet: A Novel Two-Stage Framework for Indoor Radio Map Prediction

    ICASSP 2025

    Xin Li, Ran Liu, Saihua Xu, Sirajudeen Gulam Razul, Chau Yuen

    A two-stage framework for indoor radio-map prediction: placed 4th in the Indoor Pathloss Prediction Challenge, at 9.73 dB RMSE.
