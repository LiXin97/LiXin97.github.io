Research

Three threads, one requirement: the answer has to be checkable.

LLM Evaluation

Benchmarks built to be audited rather than trusted: per-problem provenance, verifier-checkable answers, and explicit limits on what a score supports.

Post-training

RL, on-policy distillation and verifier-guided selection, aimed at reasoning where the reward can be checked rather than guessed.

LLM Agents

Protocols, memory and measurement for systems where several models interact — and whether interacting helps at all.
