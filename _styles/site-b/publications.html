Year

2026

Nine peer-reviewed papers and one preprint. First or joint-first author on six of the ten.

  1. NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track, poster Position: first of 7

    WirelessMathBench-XL: An Auditable Benchmark for Wireless Mathematical Reasoning

    Xin Li, Mengbing Liu, Yiyang Zhu, Wenhe Zhang, Li Wei, Jiancheng An, Chau Yuen

    4,027 problems drawn from 836 arXiv papers across 20 wireless subfields. Every problem ships 13-gram contamination-overlap evidence against public pretraining text. Frontier models cluster at 86.5–91.3%.

    ScopeThe contamination claim was deliberately narrowed during review. It is a rerunnable audit producing per-problem evidence for one lexical channel — not proof that the benchmark is clean.

    Project page arXiv 2509.23219

  2. NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track, poster Position: joint first of 3

    Measuring Collapse and Correction in Homogeneous-Panel LLM Debate

    DebateLedger

    Xin Li∗, Mengbing Liu∗, Chau Yuen

    Across 6,925 logged MMLU-Pro debates, 253 collapses — a correct majority turned wrong. A probe-gated freeze prevents 29 collapses but gives up 108 corrections: net −79 under equal weights. 58.9% of collapses begin in the first debate round.

    Project page

  3. ICLR 2026 Position: joint first of 5

    Local Success Does Not Compose: Benchmarking Large Language Models for Compositional Formal Verification

    DafnyComp

    Xu Xu∗, Xin Li∗, Xingwei Qu, Jie Fu, Binhang Yuan

    A model can verify a function on its own and still fail once the specifications have to compose. The benchmark measures the distance between those two results.

    Project page OpenReview arXiv 2509.23061

  4. arXiv preprint 2026 Position: first of 9

    Beyond Teacher Assignment: Domain-Normalized Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

    Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Xin Gao, Annan Wang, Yuchen Xie, Jinghao Guo, Xingwei Qu, Yichi Zhang, Chau Yuen

    Label routing decides which teacher supervises a prompt but not how strongly that teacher’s feedback counts; normalizing the feedback scale per domain adds 1.2–3.1 points over label routing, across 2B, 4B and 9B students.

    Project page arXiv 2609.35347 Code Data Models

  5. Findings of EMNLP 2026 Position: first of 3

    Target-Local Verifier Choice in Best-of-K Reasoning Selection

    TLVC

    Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Weisi Lin

    On a 34-generator, 7-verifier Best-of-K math panel, one strong process reward model is the best fixed verifier overall — and still not the best verifier for every generator. Label-free candidate statistics predict which verifier class wins.

    Code no preprint yet

    Stacked Intelligent Metasurface-Diffractive Deep Neural Networks for Onboard Terrain Classification from SAR Level-0 Raw Data
