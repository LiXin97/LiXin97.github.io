Beyond Teacher Assignment: Domain-Normalized Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Xin Gao, Annan Wang, Yuchen Xie, Jinghao Guo, Xingwei Qu, Yichi Zhang, Chau Yuen

Label routing decides which teacher supervises a prompt, but not how strongly that teacher's feedback counts. Normalizing the feedback scale per domain adds 1.2–3.1 points over label routing, across 2B, 4B and 9B students.

Preprint 2026

Many Right Answers, One Training Example: Removing Responder Noise from GRPO for LLM Recommenders

Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Jinghao Guo, Weisi Lin, Chau Yuen

Rank-GRPO trains an LLM recommender on one human answer at a time, so 85% of its advantage variance records which answer was drawn. Rewarding each list once against all answers reaches the baseline's recall in 25–53% of the RL steps, across seven backbones and three datasets.

ErrOPD: When Does On-Policy Distillation Transfer Teacher Errors?

Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Annan Wang, Yichi Zhang, Chau Yuen

ErrOPD is a controlled error-injection testbed for on-policy distillation. Forward-KL OPD transmits an injected teacher error within 42 steps; reverse-KL OPD delays it rather than blocks it.

TMLR 2026

Re:Form: Reducing Human Annotations in Scalable Formal Software Verification with RL in LLMs: A Preliminary Study on Dafny

Chuanhao Yan*, Fengdi Che*, Xuhan Huang*, Xu Xu*, Xin Li*, Yizhi Li*, Xingwei Qu*, …, Jie Fu

Trains 0.5B–14B models to write Dafny specifications with minimal human annotation — no natural-language chain-of-thought, auto-generated training data, rewards from the Dafny verifier; on 300 out-of-domain DafnyComp programs, the 14B RL model passes the verifier on 14.0% at pass@1, against 8.3% after SFT alone and 2.7% for Claude, the model that generated its training data.

npj Wireless Technology 2026

Reconstructing wireless signals for low altitude networks using small language models

Xin Li, Ran Liu, Chau Yuen

Small language models can learn physical reasoning from rewards. Fine-tuned with GRPO and physics-informed rewards, a 0.5B model reconstructs RSSI along UAV flight paths at 2.85 dB mean absolute error, 49% below its untrained baseline and on par with GPT-4o, transfers zero-shot to spatial interpolation, and runs at 3 ms latency.

EMNLP 2026 Industry

GraphReduce: Coverage-Preserving LLM Aggregation for E-commerce Review Insights

Hao Jiang, Xin Li, Yichi Zhang, Weisi Lin

Turns thousands of review tuples into a ranked list of product insights while every tuple stays attached to the insight it supports: membership is computed outside the LLM, so coverage is 100% with no duplicates. An anti-copy reinforcement objective lifts the upstream extractor from 59.3% to 93.1% production quality.
