RINGThe loop drawn as a ring, centred on the page

Three nodes clockwise, a dot running round it, the return leg from 03 to 01 in the accent colour. The centre holds the arc of the work: so far where an answer can be checked, now where it can't.
TRACKThree panels left to right, a return rail underneath

Each result sits inside the leg it came from, so the loop is also the evidence. On a phone the rail runs down the left edge.
SCROLLA scroll story with the ring pinned beside it

Each step lights its node as you reach it; the last step, the loop run by the agent itself, lights the whole ring and sets it turning.

framing: the visitor has thirty seconds and is deciding whether this is worth an hour
C6 pages · Loop / Measure / Train / Compose / Record

The nav is the cycle. Each leg page ends by handing you the next leg's question; Compose sends you back to Measure. Drawn as a real ring with the checkable domains at its centre.

framing: make the shape of the research land, not the list of outputs

framing: the site itself is a piece of craft this person made

Single pages — built from the research

D1Artifact-first

The bio names the artifacts, not the role. One "Selected work" list ordered by consequence rather than date. No news, no sections, no chrome — all the weight on the writing.

after Shunyu Yao (OpenAI) and Noam Brown (OpenAI)
D2Refined academic

Keeps your current type system, deletes the rail and the scroll-spy, raises the craft. The roadmap appears once, quiet and legible, as the only figure on the page.

after Danqi Chen (Princeton) — the one page in a 21-page survey that looks designed and still reads serious
D3Roadmap-led

The loop is the page's spine, not a section of it. Work is organised by thread — Measure, Train, Compose — instead of by year. The most distinctive and the riskiest.

no precedent in the survey; built from the recommendation that the one image should be the loop
D4Claims, not citations

The page is a reverse-chronological list of what each piece of work established — date, one sentence with a number, venue, links. Scannable end to end in under a minute.

after Jason Wei (Meta MSL) and Ethan Perez (Anthropic)
