How to read it

The other three pages argue a position and only cite what the argument needs. This one does the opposite: every publication in reverse order, the complete author list each time, and my own place in it stated rather than implied. An asterisk marks equal contribution.

Author position is given because it is the part of a citation that a reader cannot reconstruct and that an author can easily let slide. Eighth of thirteen is eighth of thirteen.
