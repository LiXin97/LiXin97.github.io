I work on making large language models reason reliably: benchmarks that measure whether they do, post-training that teaches them to, and agentic systems that test whether coordination actually helps. Most of it lands in domains where an answer can be checked — formal verification, mathematics, and code.

research

llm evaluation — benchmarks built to be audited rather than trusted: per-problem provenance, verifier-checkable answers, explicit limits on what a score supports.
post-training — rl, on-policy distillation and verifier-guided selection, aimed at reasoning where the reward can be checked rather than guessed.
llm agents — protocols, memory and measurement for systems where several models interact.
