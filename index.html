I currently work on large language models and agent frameworks. I am interested in developing Large Language Model-based tools and agent frameworks to automate mathematically rigorous tasks in wireless system design, robotic perception & SLAM, and AI-driven engineering workflows.

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Chau Yuen. Previously, I obtained my Master's degree at Peking University and Bachelor's degree at Northeastern University, CN.
