About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), advised by Prof. Chau Yuen. My research focuses on large language models and agent frameworks, with an emphasis on automating mathematically rigorous tasks in wireless system design, robotic perception & SLAM, and AI-driven engineering workflows.

I received my Master of Engineering from Peking University and my Bachelor's degree from Northeastern University, China.
